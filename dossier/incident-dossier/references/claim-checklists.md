# Claim checklists

These are generic fallback lists, not legal or insurance advice. Dossier uses one only when there is no claim document, or when a document could not be read and the user says use generic or asks to save the checklist. Your policy, lease, carrier or local rules may ask for something else, and no list here promises that a claim will succeed.

This file is plain Markdown, so you can edit it to suit. Each bullet becomes one required item. Every deadline reads: not known, check your policy.

## Auto insurance

- Notice of the loss to your insurer
- Claim form
- Policy number
- Date, time and location of the incident
- Photographs of the damage
- Video or other recording of the incident
- Name and contact details of any other party, and the plate of any other vehicle
- Repair estimate
- Police report number, if a report was made

## Renters or homeowners insurance

- Notice of the loss to your insurer
- Claim form or proof of loss
- Policy number
- Date, time and location of the incident
- Photographs of the damage or the scene
- Video or other recording of the incident
- List of damaged or stolen items, with their value
- Receipts or other proof of ownership
- Repair estimate
- Police report number, for a theft or break-in

## Package carrier

- Tracking number
- Order number and the seller's contact details
- Delivery date and time the carrier shows
- Delivery address
- Description and value of the contents
- Receipt or other proof of value
- Photographs of the delivery spot or the damaged packaging
- Video or other recording of the delivery spot
- Claim form. Check whether the sender or the recipient has to file it

## Landlord dispute

This list is general, and the rules depend on where you live. Check your local tenant rules.

- Copy of the lease
- Landlord or manager contact details
- Date and description of the issue
- Written notice to the landlord, and any reply
- Photographs of the issue, or of the condition at move-in and move-out
- Video or other recording of the issue
- Rent or deposit payment records
- Repair requests and the responses to them

## Police report

This list is general, and the process depends on where you live. Check with your local police.

- Report number and the agency that holds it
- Date, time and location of the incident
- Description of what happened
- List of what was taken or damaged, with its value
- Description of any people or vehicles seen, and the plate of any vehicle. Dossier names no stranger, so report only what you or your cameras actually recorded
- Photographs of the damage or the scene
- Video or other recording of the incident
